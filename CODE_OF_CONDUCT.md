# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies by reference. The summary below states how it applies here.

## Our standard

We want a project where people can learn, disagree and build together without fear.
Expected behaviour: respectful language, constructive review, credit for others' work,
and care for people who are newer, less represented or less confident than you.
Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other
people's private information, sexualised content, trolling, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

It applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events) and when
you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainers listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md), using the
conduct channel named there. Reports are handled privately. Do not post the private details
of anyone in a public thread.

## Enforcement

The maintainers review each report and decide on a response, from a private correction to a
permanent ban. They may act on reports of conduct outside project spaces when it affects the
project. Anyone involved in a report must not retaliate.
